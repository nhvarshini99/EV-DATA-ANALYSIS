# Electric Vehicle Population Data Analysis 🚗⚡

## Overview
This project focuses on analyzing Electric vehicle (EV) population data to understand adoption trends, geographic distribution and key vehicle characteristics. The goal is to generate data-driven insights that can support policy decisions, infrastructure planning and market analysis related to electric vehicles.

## Dataset
The dataset contains over 90,000 EV records with attributes including:
- Vehicle make, model and model year
- Electric vehicle type
- Electric range
- Clean Alternative Fuel Vehicle (CAFV) eligibility
- Geographic details (county, city, latitude, longitude)

## Key Business Questions
- What are the most common EV makes and models?
- How is EV adoption distributed across counties?
- How has EV adoption changed over model years?
- How does electric range vary by make and model?
- What proportion of vehicles are CAFV eligible?
- Are there noticeable regional (urban vs rural) adoption patterns?

## Analysis & Approach
- Data cleaning and preprocessing
- Parsing and handling geospatial location data
- Aggregation and trend analysis using pandas
- Visual exploration through bar charts, line plots, pie charts, and maps

## Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas, NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Folium (for geospatial visualization)
- Google Colab

## Key Insights
- EV adoption has increased significantly in recent years
- Urban counties show higher EV concentration than rural areas
- Newer EV models generally offer higher electric range
- A majority of vehicles qualify for CAFV incentives

## Conclusion
The analysis highlights strong growth in electric vehicle adoption, driven by technological improvements and supportive policies. Expanding charging infrastructure and incentives, particularly in rural regions, can further accelerate EV adoption.
